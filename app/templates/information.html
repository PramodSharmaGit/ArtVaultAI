Everything you need to get started with a little creative time.

What ages are the activities for?

Our activities cover ages 2–12. Each activity lists a suggested age range and materials. Use the catalog’s age filter to find a starting point for your child.

Do I need an account to browse?

No. Everyone can browse the catalog and activity details. You need to sign in to download an activity.

What does a free account include?

A free account can download free-tier activities up to a lifetime limit of {{ download_cap }} downloads. This allowance does not reset monthly.

What does membership include?

Members can download both free and member activities without a download limit. The current membership upgrade is a demonstration and requires no payment.

What will I download?

This demo currently provides placeholder text files for the six catalog activities. Full printable activity files are not yet available.

How do I find the materials I need?

The materials appear on each activity card and detail page. Choose an activity that suits the supplies you have, and read our activity safety guidance before getting started.

Make room for creativity with thoughtful preparation and adult supervision.

Create together

Activities are intended for children with adult supervision. Suggested ages are a guide; choose materials and tasks that suit your child’s abilities.

Check your supplies

Follow the age labels and instructions on art supplies. Keep small cut-outs and loose decorations away from children who may put them in their mouths. An adult should help with cutting and handle sharp tools.

Prepare the space

Use a stable work surface, protect the table and keep supplies within easy reach. For window activities, adults should place artwork at a reachable height so children do not need to climb.

Choose materials thoughtfully

Consider known sensitivities when choosing glue, plants and other supplies. Keep craft materials separate from food, and tidy up and wash hands when you finish.
